# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age,
body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level
of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion,
or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy
community.

## Expected behavior

- Be empathetic and respectful.
- Give and accept constructive feedback.
- Focus on what is best for the community and responsible defensive security.
- Respect privacy, consent, and authorized-testing boundaries.
- Take responsibility, apologize, and learn when harm occurs.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, doxxing, sexualized attention, deliberate disruption, and publishing
another person's private information without permission are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's maintainer contact or private security advisory channel.
Maintainers will review reports fairly, protect reporters' privacy where possible, and may remove content, issue a
warning, impose a temporary restriction, or permanently remove a participant when appropriate.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone officially represents the project.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html).
